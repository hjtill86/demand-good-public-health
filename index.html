The Problem

Most public health training wasn't built for busy practitioners.

Too academic. Too generic. Too slow.

  • Semester-long courses that don't fit around a full-time job
  • Theory-heavy content with no ready-to-use deliverables
  • Generic templates that need to be rebuilt from scratch
  • No community to ask questions or benchmark against

Short, practical, field-tested.

  • 10–25 minute micro-lessons you can finish on a lunch break
  • Templates and tools you can use the same day
  • Built by practitioners, for practitioners
  • A real community and ongoing support
