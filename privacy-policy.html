Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

DemandGoodQA ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Demand Good Public Health website and the associated Thinkific learning portal. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site or enroll in our courses and membership plans.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name and email address, when you submit a contact form or newsletter sign-up.
  • Account and payment information collected by our learning platform, Thinkific, when you enroll in a course or membership plan.
  • Basic usage data (such as pages visited) collected automatically to help us improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to purchased courses, templates, and membership content.
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact page or email.
  • To send newsletters, product updates, and community announcements, where you have opted in.
  • To improve our website, courses, and templates.

Third-Party Services

Course enrollment, payment processing, and content delivery are handled by Thinkific, a third-party learning management platform. Your use of the Thinkific portal is also governed by Thinkific's own privacy policy and terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link provided in each email.
  • You may request that we delete personal information you have submitted directly to us by contacting support@demandgoodqa.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@demandgoodqa.com.
