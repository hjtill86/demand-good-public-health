About DemandGoodQA

Practical training, built from the ground up in Gary, Indiana.

We believe public health professionals deserve skills training that respects their time and delivers real, usable tools — not just theory.

🎯

Our Mission

To equip public health professionals, students, and organizations with practical micro-skills and ready-to-use tools that improve real-world public health practice — quickly, affordably, and without the fluff.

💡

Our Vision

A public health workforce where every practitioner — regardless of budget, geography, or job title — has access to field-tested training and tools that make good public health work possible everywhere.

HT
Founder Story

Why Henry Tillman started DemandGoodQA.

Henry Tillman spent years working inside public health systems, watching talented professionals get stuck rebuilding the same assessments, logic models, and reports from scratch — often without the training or tools to do it efficiently. Traditional public health education helped build foundational knowledge, but it rarely translated into the practical, day-to-day skills the job actually requires.

DemandGoodQA was created to close that gap: short, practical lessons paired with a growing vault of ready-to-use templates, built specifically for the realities of public health work.

Why Public Health Micro-Learning Matters

Public health professionals are stretched thin. Long courses and dense textbooks don't fit around the demands of the job. Micro-learning — short, focused lessons that teach one practical skill at a time — lets practitioners build real competency in the time they actually have, whether that's a lunch break or a commute.

Why Gary, Indiana Matters

Gary, Indiana has faced real public health challenges — from environmental exposure to economic disinvestment — and its communities have had to demand good public health for decades. DemandGoodQA is rooted in that same spirit: practical, community-centered, and unwilling to settle for training that doesn't translate into action.
