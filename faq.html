FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about membership, access, certificates, and how DemandGoodQA works.

The Micro-Skill Lab is a library of short, self-paced lessons (10–25 minutes each) that teach practical public health skills like community health assessment, epidemiology fundamentals, program evaluation, grant writing, logic models, data visualization, health equity, and PHAB preparation.

The Template Vault is a growing collection of field-tested templates and tools — including CHNA templates, logic model worksheets, dashboards, surveys, grant narrative templates, reporting tools, and meeting templates — ready to download and customize.

All memberships are delivered through our learning portal on Thinkific. After choosing a plan, you'll create (or log into) your Thinkific account and get instant access to your courses and downloads.

Yes. Micro-Skill Lab members earn a certificate of completion for each lesson track, which you can use to document professional development.

New lessons and templates are added monthly, so your membership keeps growing the longer you're enrolled.

The Practitioner Plan includes full access to the Micro-Skill Lab. The Vault Pro Plan includes everything in the Practitioner Plan plus full access to the Template Vault.
